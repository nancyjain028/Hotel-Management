# 🏨 Hotel Management System

## 📖 About the Project

The **Hotel Management System** is a software application designed to simplify and organize common hotel management activities. It helps manage important hotel operations such as **customer information, room details, room availability, bookings, reservations, check-in, and check-out**.

The main purpose of this project is to reduce manual record keeping and provide an organized way to manage hotel-related information. The system helps hotel staff maintain customer and room records and manage booking-related activities efficiently.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop a simple and efficient hotel management solution that makes it easier to manage daily hotel operations.

The project aims to:

* Reduce manual record keeping
* Manage customer information efficiently
* Maintain room details and availability
* Manage room bookings and reservations
* Handle check-in and check-out information
* Organize hotel records in one place
* Provide a simple and user-friendly interface

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Customer Management

* Add and manage customer information
* Maintain customer records
* View customer details

### 🛏️ Room Management

* Manage room details
* Check room availability
* Maintain room-related information

### 📅 Booking & Reservation

* Manage room bookings
* Maintain reservation details
* Track booking information

### 🔑 Check-In & Check-Out

* Manage customer check-in details
* Manage customer check-out details
* Maintain stay-related information

### 📊 Record Management

* Organize hotel records
* Easily access customer and room information
* Reduce paperwork and manual data management
